Rules

Sound

Everyday English words count; rare words that only word-game players know don't. A full row also clears, but only words score. Some pieces carry a letter that finishes a word already on the board. Vowels come in 1–3 per piece. Q arrives as Qu. A tile marked is a blank that can be any letter. Hold Down to drop faster. Tile colours follow the letter: vowels are yellow, and the rest go from blue to green, pink and purple as they are worth more.

Every game you play here is saved for review: each piece's letters, where it went, how long it took, and the words it made. Only you and people you make editors of this page can read it.

How to play

Spell words across

Line up letters left to right in any row. A word of 3 or more letters pops and scores, and the tiles above drop into the gap.

Tap the buttons to try them

Steer each piece

  • Rotate turns the piece, and its letters turn with it.
  • Left and Right move it. Hold to keep going.
  • Hold Down to drop faster.
  • The dashed outline shows where it will land.

Longer words, bigger points

The longer the word, the bigger the points.

Every 10 words, you go up a level.

Good to know

  • A star tile is a blank: it can be any letter.
  • Q always comes as Qu.
  • A full row clears too, but scores nothing.
  • Don't let the stack reach the top!
