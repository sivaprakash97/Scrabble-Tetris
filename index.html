Scrabble-Tetris

Spell words left to right in any row, or top to bottom in any column. Any word of 3 or more letters disappears, and every tile drops to fill the gaps below. A full row also clears, but only words score.

Rules

Everyday English words count; rare Scrabble-only words don't. A full row also clears, but only words score. Some pieces carry a letter that finishes a word already on the board. Vowels come in 1–3 per piece. Q arrives as Qu. A tile marked is a blank that can be any letter. Hold Down to drop faster.

Every game you play here is saved for review: each piece's letters, where it went, how long it took, and the words it made. Only you and people you make editors of this page can read it.
