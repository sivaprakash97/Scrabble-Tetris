Rules

Everyday English words count; rare Scrabble-only words don't. A full row also clears, but only words score. Letters follow English letter frequency: E, T, A and O turn up most. After two rare letters (V, K, J, X, Q, Z) in quick succession, rare letters are held back for a few tiles. Q arrives as Qu. Hold Down to drop faster.

Every game you play here is saved for review: each piece's letters, where it went, how long it took, and the words it made. Only you and people you make editors of this page can read it.
